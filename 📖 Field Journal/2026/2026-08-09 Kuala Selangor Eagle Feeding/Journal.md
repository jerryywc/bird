# Kuala Selangor Eagle Feeding

## Summary

Classic morning boat session — dense WBSE and Brahminy Kite action. Best keeper was an adult sea eagle banking with mangrove backdrop. Bonus: smooth otter near the bank on the return.

## Field notes

- Boat positioned well under the main flight path
- Juveniles present — good ID practice
- Crowd on boat was light; easy to rotate shooting lanes

## Photography notes

- **Keepers:** WBSE_001 (bank with catch), BK_001 (dive peak)
- **Misses / lessons:** clipped wings on two close passes — leave more AF-area margin
- **Settings that worked:** 1/2500, f/7.1, ISO 640–1000, EFCS off, electronic shutter
